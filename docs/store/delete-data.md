# Delete your data — Pit Wall

**App:** Pit Wall · **Developer:** Kumar Veetrag

Pit Wall has no account of its own, so there is no account to delete. This
page explains how to remove each piece of data the game touches.

## Game progress on your phone

Settings, the current season and your career record live in the app's
private storage. **Uninstall the app** (or use Android Settings → Apps →
Pit Wall → Storage → Clear storage) and all of it is deleted immediately.

## Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you were signed in to Play Games, a copy of your career is kept in
Google's Saved Games. To delete it:

1. Open the **Google Play Games** app.
2. Go to your profile → **Settings** → **Delete Play Games account & data**,
   or find **Pit Wall** under your games and delete its saved data.

This deletes the save permanently. Nothing else is kept.

## Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Crash reports contain the crash, the device model and Android version, and
a random installation ID. They are not linked to your name, email or Play
Games account. They are **deleted automatically after 90 days**.

To have them deleted sooner, email **veetrag@gmail.com** with the subject
"Pit Wall data deletion" and, if you can, the date of the crash and your
phone model. Matching reports will be deleted within 30 days. Uninstalling
the app also resets the installation ID, so new reports cannot be linked to
old ones.
