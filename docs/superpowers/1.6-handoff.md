circuits); no AI stop on the final lap; a new career's first race is fully dry;

## Calls I made for you (what each costs if wrong)

Balance / feel — check on the phone:
- AI weather confirm 2 laps, no weather stop with ≤3 laps left. Cost: AI sits
  out short damp spells on slicks and loses time.
- First career race fully dry; later round-1s keep only a dry first lap.
- Slow stops 4% before crew relief; `pitCrewSlowRelief` raised 0.5 → 0.8 so Apex
  stays the strongest team (the locked balance check). Cost: top crews rarely
  have slow stops.
- Radio requests: box at 85% cliff, push after 2 lap-ends ≤1.0s behind a slower
  car, 6-lap cooldown, 3/race, 8s expiry (paused while the race is paused).
  YES acts at once. Push streak resets under any safety car/VSC.
- Forecast: up to 10 laps ahead; the window always contains the true lap; the AI
  does not use it; the projection still sees true weather.
- Red flag: 5% per crash retirement, max 1/race, not in the last 5 laps. Restart
  order = order at the flag; wet restart gives everyone the weather tyre; a dry
  restart fits the next planned tyre if due within 15 laps (and uses that stop).
  PIT is disabled from crash to card. The restart's free change counts in the
  results' pit-stop count if it used a planned stop.
- Safety-car rate: one measuring run came out 0.392 vs its 0.40 band floor (the
  enforced checks pass) — red flags take the place of some safety cars.
- Team offers: board target = your car's deserved constructors' rank, no penalty
  for missing it. Offer if you beat it by ≥2 places or win the title, from the
  team 2 places higher by rating. Accept: pick one of its two drivers; car
  upgrades stay with the old team; development points and wing setups move
  with you. This reverses the career design's "player never changes team".
- Large text = 15% zoom of tower, pit panel, banners, rival card; compact tower
  shows 5 rows. Row slide animation may start slightly off under zoom.
- Share card always uses the standard tyre colours.
- Scenarios (all re-picked from real seeds, goal = one better than the AI):
  Recovery Drive P10 → P6 (Bengaluru); Cliff Edge P3 → win (Harbour Lights);
  Rain Call P3 → win, 33 laps (Silverpark); VSC Gamble: you *lead* on tired
  inters under a VSC → hold P1 (Aurora — the midfield version found no seed);
  Sprint Finish P5 → P2; Red Flag Restart P4 → P3 (Monsoon Bay).
- What-if: shows the lower middle of 8 futures, the range, and "better in N of
  8"; a red flag's free change is not listed as an editable stop.
