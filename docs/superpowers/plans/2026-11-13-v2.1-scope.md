## 0. Goal, version, in/out

**Goal.** Tyres get a temperature. Fresh sets come out of the pits cold (hards take most of a lap to come in, softs almost none). Pushing on a hot track overheats them, so they lose pace and wear faster. Softs and mediums run cold on a green track grain for a few laps. Smart and optimal AI cars manage temperature. The player sees a COLD/IN WINDOW/HOT band on the tyre meter, COLD tags on the tower, the out-lap price for each compound in the pit panel, radio lines, and a coach mark.

**Version:** **2.1 / versionCode 14** (2.0 is 13).

**In:** plan Task 5 (thermal part 1), Task 6 (overheating wear, graining, AI thermal pace), Task 7 (on screen), one switch-on/measure task, and the release.

**Out (owner has REJECTED these; do not build them):** team orders, the planner, "2.2". Also out: fuel, ERS, the PU sheet, FUEL LOAD, circuit/wet incident risk (it stays OFF as 2.0 shipped it), and the penalties `hotFactor` (see Task 5 §5.9).

**Rules carried from the plan and the 2.0 scope, unchanged:** Global Constraints L33–L44 (no new `rng()`; golden race never re-captured; new fields absent when off and appended last; every number in `tuning.json` + zod + `TuningConstants`), Conventions L82–L91 (band rule, scenario procedure, switch-on procedure), commit trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
