# Plan A — race

## 0. Weather fixes (do first)

### 0.1 AI weather-pit churn

**Problem:** `weatherPit` (`strategy.ts:33-40`) boxes every AI car the lap the
track turns DAMP and again the lap it turns DRY. DAMP has no minimum duration,
so showery circuits produce ~100 AI stops per GP.

**Fix (AI side only — the weather generator is unchanged, so the timeline, the
player's view and every seed's weather stay identical):**
- A dry-tyred car switches to wet-family only when the track has been non-DRY
  for `ai.weatherConfirmLaps` (start **2**) consecutive completed laps, or is
  WET now. A wet-tyred car switches to slicks only when it has been DRY for
  `ai.weatherConfirmLaps` consecutive laps.
- No weather stop when `lapsRemaining ≤ ai.weatherMinLapsLeft` (start **3**).
- Consecutive-lap counts come from `state.weatherTimeline[0..state.lap]` (the
  past only — the AI never reads the future).
- Final-lap guard: `aiDecision` is not run on the boundary that completes the
  race (`newLap >= totalLaps`), for any reason.
- New band `aiWeatherStopsPerRace` measured on the showery circuits; the
  existing winner/overtake/SC bands must hold. The AI will now sit out short
  damp spells on slicks and lose time — that is the intended trade.

**Call:** 2 confirm laps / 3 laps left are first guesses.

### 0.2 A new player's first race is fully dry

**Call (settles Veet's open question):** the very first career race (season 1,
round 1) has a fully dry weather timeline. Later round-1s keep today's
`dryStart` (dry opening lap only). Implemented as `RaceConfig.allDry?: true`,
set by `buildRaceConfig` when `seasonNumber === 1 && raceIndex === 0`;
`openingTimeline` returns all DRY when set, consuming the same rng draws so
nothing downstream shifts. Qualifying for that round reads the same flag.
Race setup's forecast agrees (existing `setupWeatherAgrees` tests extended).

## 1. Pit stop variance

- `pitStopTime` already draws a `gaussianPair` and discards `g2`. A stop is
  **slow** when `g2 > Φ⁻¹(1 − pit.slowStopChance)` — no extra rng draw, so the
  stream stays aligned. Slow time added = `pit.slowStopMin + (pit.slowStopMax −
  pit.slowStopMin) × frac`, where `frac` is derived deterministically from `g2`
  (e.g. `(Φ(g2) − (1−p)) / p`). Start values: chance **0.04**, 2.0–6.0 s.
  `pitCrew` facet relief: chance × `(1 − crew × pit.pitCrewSlowRelief)` (start
  0.5). With `slowStopChance` 0 nothing changes.
- Applies to everyone (AI, plan stops, the PIT button).
- The projection prices the **expected** stop (no slow roll): `pitStopTime`
  gains an option `{ allowSlow: false }` used by `projectPitNow`.
- `pitStopTime` returns `{ total, slow: number }`, or a sibling function returns
  the slow component; callers that add to raceTime use `total`.
- The pit timer shows the real stop: raceStore latches the actual stop time
  (`lastStopTime`) at every player stop; `PitTimer` animates that, and turns hot
  with "SLOW STOP +3.4s" when slow.
- Radio line `slow_stop` for the player; commentary line for a rival's slow
  stop that costs a position (new `IncidentKind` `slow_stop`, logged with
  driverId and timeLoss).
- Bands: existing bands must hold; add `slowStopsPerRace`.

## 2. Driver radio requests

- The driver occasionally asks a yes/no question. Two kinds:
  - **Box request** — "Tyres are gone, can we box?": the player's
    `cliffFrac ≥ radioRequests.boxCliffFrac` (start **0.85**), no planned stop
    within 2 laps, `lapsRemaining > 3`, not in the pit, no request this stint.
  - **Push request** — "I'm faster than {ahead}, let me push": gap to the car
    ahead ≤ `radioRequests.pushGap` (start **1.0 s**) for 2 consecutive lap
    ends, pace mode not PUSH, `cliffFrac < 0.5`, the player's lap time over the
    last lap at least 0.1 s faster than the car ahead's.
- Detection is a pure function of race state (`src/state/radioRequests.ts`), so
  it is deterministic and testable; a per-kind cooldown of
  `radioRequests.cooldownLaps` (start **6**) and a cap of
  `radioRequests.maxPerRace` (start **3**).
- UI: a radio-request card above the HUD alert (`RadioRequest.tsx`): the line,
  YES (volt) / NO buttons, a thin countdown bar. It does **not** pause the
  race. It expires after `radioRequests.expirySec` real seconds (start **8**) =
  NO. Haptic on arrival; screen-reader announcement.
- **Call:** YES acts in one tap: box → `pitNow(nextCompound)` where
  `nextCompound` is the next planned stop's compound, else the best dry
  compound by laps remaining (the AI's `bestDryCompound`); push →
  `setPaceMode('push')`. Both are the existing logged calls, so replay and the
  debrief need no change. NO does nothing (no log entry).
- Data: `radio.json` gains `request_box`, `request_push` pools; picking a line
  stays outside the engine.
- Coexists with the pit panel: a box request is suppressed while the pit panel
  is open; opening the panel dismisses a pending box request.

## 3. Weather forecast confidence

- Replace the strip's perfect 4-lap read-out with a forecast:
  `forecast(state, config, horizon) → { kind: 'rain'|'drying'|'steady',
  fromLap, toLap, chance }` in `src/engine/forecast.ts`, pure, no rng.
  - Finds the next change in the true timeline within `weather.forecastHorizon`
    laps (start **10**).
  - The window is honest but imprecise: true change lap ± a half-width that
    grows with distance (`weather.forecastSpread`, start `[0,1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,4,4]`
    by laps ahead), shifted by a deterministic offset hashed from (seed,
    change lap) so it doesn't jitter between laps; always contains the true lap.
  - `chance` from `weather.forecastConfidence` extended to the horizon
    (start `[1,1,.9,.85,.8,.75,.7,.65,.6,.55,.5]`), rounded to 5%.
  - Beyond the horizon: "steady".
- UI: `WeatherStrip` shows "RAIN IN 6–9 LAPS · 60%" / "DRYING IN 3–5 LAPS ·
  80%" / "NO CHANGE EXPECTED"; the current-lap chip stays truthful. The old
  unused `getWeatherForecast` is removed.
- The pit panel's wet-compound offer keeps reading the true next 2 laps
  (unchanged). The projection keeps reading the true timeline (documented).
- **Call:** the AI stays reactive (it does not read the forecast).

## 4. Red flag

- **Trigger:** on a tick where a car retires by **crash**, roll red at
  `incidents.redFlag.crashChance` (start **0.05**) **before** the SC roll (with
  0 chance: no draw, SC/VSC stream unchanged). Max `redFlag.maxPerRace` 1. Not
  in the last `redFlag.minLapsLeft` (start **5**) laps.
- **Stoppage (engine):** `SafetyCarState.kind` gains `'red'`. At the next lap
  boundary the race is suspended: the engine performs the restart transform in
  one step:
  - order frozen as it stands; `raceTime` re-gridded to leader time +
    `(pos−1) × qualifying.gridSlotGapSec` (the start-grid gap);
  - every car gets a **free tyre change** (no stop time, `pitsDone` unchanged,
    tyre age 0, compound pushed to `compoundsUsed`). AI: fits its next planned
    compound if a stop is planned within `redFlag.planWindowLaps` (start 15)
    and then marks that planned stop done (`pitsDone + 1`, no time), otherwise
    fresh tyres of its current compound (wet-family by weather);
  - the player's choice comes from a new `PlayerCall` kind
    `{ step, kind: 'restart_tyre', compound }` applied at that boundary; if
    absent, the player gets the AI rule. Same plan-consumption rule as the AI.
  - incidents `red_flag` (deploy) and `red_flag_restart`; the neutralisation is
    then cleared (no SC laps after the restart).
- **Stoppage (UI):** when the engine reports a pending red flag at a boundary,
  `raceStore` enters phase `'red_flag'`: the clock stops; a full-screen RED
  FLAG card (hot) shows the frozen order (top 6 + you), "Free tyre change",
  compound chips (allocation-limited, wet only when non-DRY), and RESTART
  (volt). Confirm logs `restart_tyre` and moves to `'starting'`, so the existing
  start lights run a standing restart (then `'racing'`). Skip-start setting
  applies. Back button inert. Resume after an app kill: the race isn't
  persisted mid-race today (resumes to setup) — unchanged.
- The lap chart draws a red-flag lap as a hot band; the banner, wash and radio
  get red-flag variants; commentary lines.
- Scenarios and the debrief replay reproduce it via the call log.
- Band `redFlagPerRace`; existing bands must hold.
- **Call:** 0.05 per crash ≈ one red flag every ~10 races.

# Plan B — around the race

## 5. "What if" replay in the debrief

- In the debrief card: TRY ANOTHER PLAN. The player edits one thing: **the lap
  of one of their stops** (± up to 10 laps, clamped to the race) and/or **its
  compound** (or, if they made no stop, "add a stop on lap N").
- It runs the same machinery as the judge: branch from a replay of the real
  race 2 laps before the earliest change, then `debrief.futures` (8)
  continuations with `futureSeed`, for both the real plan and the edited plan
  (common random numbers). Result: "Your plan: P5 (P4–P6). This plan: P3
  (P2–P5) in 6 of 8 futures." Never claims a single exact finish.
- `debriefStore` keeps the job (config, seed, calls) so the what-if can run
  after `raceStore` resets. New worker message `whatIf`; in-process runner for
  tests. Not offered for scenarios or resumed races (same as the debrief).
- Engine: `whatIf(config, seed, calls, edit) → { mine: number[], theirs:
  number[] }` in `src/engine/debrief.ts`, reusing `simulate`, `withPlayerPlan`,
  `futureSeed`.

## 6. Share a result card

- SHARE button on the result screen (GP and sprint) and the championship
  screen. Renders a 1080×1350 PNG with Canvas2D: hero band (volt, or grape on
  DNF), "P3" huge, circuit · round · season, points, the podium codes, your
  team colour slash, "PIT WALL" wordmark. Fonts: `document.fonts.load` for the
  self-hosted Big Shoulders/Barlow before drawing.
- Android: write to the cache dir via `@capacitor/filesystem`, open the share
  sheet via `@capacitor/share` (both new dependencies: no DOM-to-image path
  exists and the share sheet needs the native plugin). The manifest
  `FileProvider` with `cache-path` already exists. Web fallback: download the
  PNG.
- No data leaves the device except what the player shares; data-safety doc
  note added, no form change needed.
- `cap sync` runs from the repo root at build time (never from a worktree).

- New STATS screen from the trophy room (`seasonPhase` `career_stats`, back →

## 8. More scenarios

## 10. Colour-blind tyre palette

- Settings → "Tyre colours: Standard / High contrast". High contrast sets

  soft orange-vermilion, medium yellow, hard white, inter bluish-green, wet sky

  lightness. Letters stay. `WeatherStrip`'s hardcoded literals become tokens.
- `tokenContrast` test extended to the cb set.

## 11. Text size

- Settings → "Text size: Standard / Large". Large applies `zoom: 1.15` to the

  fewer row if needed so nothing clips.
- **Call:** whole-container scaling, not per-element (≈260 inline px sizes).

## 12. Team offers

**Call (reverses career-design decision 2 and the ladder's "the player never
changes team", at Veet's instruction to build the listed feature):**
- **Board target:** each team's target is its **deserved constructors' rank**
  from the team ladder (`deservedOrder`). Shown on the Development screen and
  season-end: "BOARD TARGET · P4 IN THE CONSTRUCTORS'". No penalty for missing
  it (no demotion, no sacking) — it is the yardstick offers use.
- **Offer rule (legible, deterministic, no rng):** at season end, after the
  ladder settles, if the player's team finished **at least
  `career.offers.beatBy` (start 2) places above its board target** in the
  constructors', or the player won the drivers' title, the team
  `career.offers.stepUp` (start 2) places higher by effective rating (ladder
  offsets included; clamped to the top team, none if already top) offers a
  seat. At most one offer per season.
- **Season end UI:** an OFFER card on the championship screen: team, its car
  rating vs yours, its board target. ACCEPT / STAY. NEXT SEASON applies the
  choice. Persisted in the career record (`pendingOffer`, cleared on decision)
  so a reload keeps it.
- **On accepting:** the player picks one of the new team's two drivers
  (`DriverSelect` reused with a "NEW TEAM" eyebrow). Team development tiers
  stay with the old team (the ladder's per-team offsets are untouched); the
  player's purchased **team** tiers reset to 0 at the new team; **driver pace**
  tiers reset (new driver); `devPoints` and `wingByCircuit` carry over (they
  are the strategist's). `selectedTeamId` and `playerDriverId` update together.
- **Not a driver market:** no AI driver changes seat, no contracts or money,
  `drivers.json` never mutates, offers only at season end, declining is
  always free.
- Career history already records team per season; the trophy room shows team
  changes. No new Play achievement (published achievements are one-way).
