# Pit Wall 1.5 — VSC, rival intel, sprint weekends, lap chart

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** draft for Veet's review
**Decisions taken by Veet (2026-10-16):** VSC split by retirement cause, AI reacts
to neutralisations; rival intel = observed facts + a fuzzy box window; 3 sprint
rounds, one qualifying sets both grids; lap chart = you + 4 nearest.
**Out of scope:** rule changes between seasons (ruled out), and everything in the
rejected list. The 1.5 bug fixes are a separate branch (`fix/1.5`), not this spec.

## Goal

Give the race more strategic texture (a second kind of neutralisation, readable
rivals, a second race format) and give the debrief a picture of *why* the race
went the way it did. Every feature must keep the golden rule: the engine stays
pure and deterministic, and every balance number lives in `data/tuning.json`.

## Global constraints

- Same seed + same inputs ⇒ identical race. New rng draws only on paths that are
  off when their tuning rate is 0, and short-circuit with no draw at 0
  (`MODEL_SPEC.md` invariants). The byte-identity golden fixture must still pass
  unchanged with the new features' chances zeroed in the fixture tuning.
- Existing saves (season `SAVE_VERSION 2`, career `1`, cloud `1`) load without
  loss. New persisted fields are additive with zod `.default()`; no version bump
  unless unavoidable, in which case a migration and a real-old-save test.
- Race Day Bold tokens only; volt = the player / call to action; caution = race
  control (SC and VSC); text on volt/caution is ink. Tabular figures on all live
  numbers. Reduced motion respected. Every new control has a screen-reader label
  and is at least 44px.
- Monte-Carlo regression bands: all existing bands still hold; new bands are
  added for VSC and AI neutralisation stops, measured and written into
  `tuning.json._regressionBands` and `MODEL_SPEC.md`.

## 1. Virtual safety car

### Behaviour

- **Trigger.** On a tick where a car newly retires (the existing hook), the roll
  becomes cause-dependent, at most one neutralisation at a time:
  - *Crash:* roll SC at `crashDeployChance` (unchanged 0.30). If no SC, roll VSC
    at `vsc.crashDeployChance` (start value 0.25).
  - *Mechanical / tyre failure:* roll SC at `mechanicalDeployChance` (unchanged
    0.12). If no SC, roll VSC at `vsc.mechanicalDeployChance` (start 0.40).
  - SC draws happen first and exactly as today, so with VSC chances at 0 the
    rng stream is untouched.
  - A VSC counts against its own `vsc.maxPerRace` (2). An SC may supersede a
    running VSC (a later crash); a VSC never supersedes an SC.
- **While it runs.** Duration uniform `vsc.durationLapsMin..Max` (1–3), counted
  down like the SC. All cars lap at `vsc.paceMultiplier` (start 1.30). **Gaps are
  frozen, not bunched** — no `compressRaceTimes`. Overtakes are suspended
  (same rule as SC). Pit lane loss is `pitLaneTimeLoss × vsc.laneLossFactor`
  (start 0.60), returned by `effectivePitLaneLoss`, so the projection, pit panel
  and pit timer stay on one source of truth.
- **State.** `SafetyCarState` gains `kind: 'sc' | 'vsc'`. Incident events gain
  `vsc_deployed` / `vsc_ended`. An absent `kind` is read as `'sc'` so nothing
  older breaks.

### AI reaction (SC and VSC)

`aiDecision` reads `state.safetyCar`. Under any neutralisation, a **smart** or
**optimal** brain boxes at the lap boundary when it has not yet made its planned
stop and either its planned stop lap is within `ai.neutralisationWindowLaps`
(start 6) or its tyre cliff fraction is at least `ai.neutralisationCliffFrac`
(start 0.55). It fits the compound its plan names for that stop. **Naive** brains
ignore neutralisations. No new rng draw: the decision is a pure threshold.

### Presentation

- Banner: "VIRTUAL SAFETY CAR · N LAPS", the same caution banner component with
  a "VSC" chip instead of the SC car glyph; haptic on deploy; screen-reader
  announcement on deploy and end.
- Tower: the same caution flash on deploy. Track canvas: a thinner caution wash
  than the SC (about half the opacity) so the two are distinguishable at a glance.
- Pit panel: "−Xs UNDER VSC" pill (the existing SC pill, relabelled).
- Commentary: new `vsc_deployed` / `vsc_ended` lines in `commentary.json`; radio
  line for the player when a VSC opens and their stop is near ("VSC, VSC — box
  this lap is cheap").

### Tests

Unit: trigger split by cause, SC-first draw order, max per race, SC supersedes
VSC, frozen gaps (no compression), lane-loss factor, AI boxes / naive does not.
Determinism and byte-identity with zeroed VSC chances. Monte-Carlo: new
`vscPerRace` band and `aiNeutralisationStopsPerRace` band; existing winner,
overtake, SC and crash bands still pass.

## 2. Rival intel

### Behaviour

- Every non-player row in the timing tower becomes tappable. Tap opens an intel
  card anchored to the bottom of the tower (does not pause the race); tapping
  the same row, another row, or outside closes or switches it. Player row: no
  card.
- The card shows: driver code + team colour slash; current compound chip and
  **tyre age in laps**; **stops made**; and one of:
  - "BOX WINDOW L18–22" while a planned stop remains,
  - "RUNNING TO THE FLAG" once the plan is complete,
  - "OUT" for a retired car.
- The window is a pure engine function `rivalIntel(state, carId)` in
  `engine/rivalIntel.ts`. Centre = the earlier of the planned stop lap and the
  lap at which the car's tyre is projected to reach its brain's cliff threshold
  (from the existing tyre model at current pace). Half-width by brain: naive 2,
  smart 3, optimal 5 laps (in `tuning.json`), clamped to the race. A small
  deterministic offset derived from a hash of the car id (not the rng) keeps the
  window honest-but-imprecise without jittering between laps. The brain itself
  is never named on screen.
- The window recomputes once per lap; the card updates in place.

### Presentation

Card is a slanted grape panel, 1 row tall plus a line, entering with the kit's
`snapIn`; the tapped row gets a lilac outline while open. Rows become buttons
with `aria-label` "Rival intel: VER, medium, 14 laps old, 1 stop, box window
lap 18 to 22". Row height unchanged.

### Tests

`rivalIntel` unit tests (window centre from plan vs cliff, widths by brain,
complete plan, retired, clamping, stable across sectors within a lap). UI: tap
opens/switches/closes, player row inert, a11y label.

## 3. Sprint weekends

### Format

- Rounds 4, 8 and 12 of the 12-round calendar are sprint weekends
  (`tuning.json` `sprint.rounds: [4, 8, 12]`, 1-based; the calendar test checks
  they exist and are not wet-leaning). Round 12 makes the finale a double header.
- Sprint distance `round(circuit.laps × sprint.distanceFraction)` (start 0.33).
  No mandatory two-compound rule. Points `sprint.points` = 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 to the
  top eight. No development points, no rival development from the sprint (those
  stay tied to the Grand Prix, so the economy is unchanged).
- **One qualifying sets both grids.** The sprint result does not set Sunday's
  grid.

### Round flow (sprint rounds only)

`race_setup` (Grand Prix plan + wing, as today) → `qualifying` →
**`sprint_setup`** (start tyre + pace only; a stop is allowed but not planned
by default) → **`sprint_racing`** → **`sprint_result`** (result, sprint points,
debrief with lap chart, RETRY SPRINT / ON TO THE GRAND PRIX) → `racing` (Grand
Prix, from the same qualifying grid) → `result` → as today.

- The sprint has its own seed salt (`seedFor(season, raceIndex, attempt, 'sprint')`)
  and its own attempt counter, so retrying the sprint never changes the Grand
  Prix weather and vice versa.
- Sprint points are banked exactly once, when the player leaves
  `sprint_result`, using the same pending/keep pattern as the Grand Prix
  (`sprintPending`). The once-only invariant gets its own test.
- Resume after the app is killed: `sprint_setup` and `sprint_racing` resume to
  `sprint_setup`; `sprint_result` resumes to `sprint_result` with the pending
  result intact. Qualifying result is persisted for sprint rounds only (it is
  needed for the Grand Prix grid after a resume).
- Standings show sprint points folded into the championship total; the round's
  standing row notes "S+6" for sprint points scored.
- Career record: sprints are logged in `raceLog` with `kind: 'sprint'`. Wins,
  poles and races-run count Grand Prix only (achievements unchanged); a new
  "sprint wins" stat appears in the trophy room.
- Menus: "SPRINT WEEKEND" pill on the main menu's next-race tile, race setup and
  qualifying for sprint rounds.

### Engine

`initRace` takes an optional laps override and a rules override (no mandatory
stop); `buildRaceConfig` passes them for sprints. Debrief replay uses the same
config, so judging and the lap chart work for sprints unchanged.

### Tests

Phase transitions, seed independence of sprint vs Grand Prix, points once-only,
resume from every new phase, career counts, calendar test, sprint distance and
no mandatory stop in the engine.

## 4. Lap chart in the debrief

### Behaviour

- Shown on the result screen (Grand Prix and sprint) above the existing debrief
  verdicts.
- Data from a pure engine function `lapHistory(config, seed, calls)` in
  `engine/raceHistory.ts`, built on the existing `replay`: positions of every
  car at the end of each lap, each car's pit-stop laps, and neutralisation
  periods (kind + start/end lap) from the incident log. No change to `RaceState`
  or the golden fixture. Runs once, off the result screen's critical path (same
  moment as the debrief judging), and is cached with the debrief result.
- Lines: the player in volt, 3px; the 4 nearest finishers (2 ahead, 2 behind,
  shifted at P1/P20) in their team colours, 2px; every other car a 1px faded
  lilac-dim line. Pit stops are dots on the line; SC laps a caution band, VSC
  laps a hatched caution band. Retired cars' lines end with an "×".
- Axes: P1 at top; lap ticks every 10 laps; labels in Barlow, tabular figures.
  A legend row of the five highlighted codes; tapping a code brings that line to
  the front.
- Motion: lines draw in left to right over ~0.6s once on entry; reduced motion
  shows them complete. Screen readers get a text summary ("Started P7, P4 after
  the stop on lap 21, finished P3").

### Tests

`lapHistory` against a known seeded race (positions per lap match live state
sampled at lap ends — `replayFidelity`-style), pit and neutralisation markers,
retired cars. UI: highlighted set selection at the edges of the grid, legend
tap, reduced motion, text summary.

## Balance calls flagged for Veet

All start values above (VSC chances 0.25 / 0.40, VSC pace 1.30, lane factor
0.60, AI window 6 laps / cliff 0.55, intel widths 2/3/5, sprint fraction 0.33,
rounds 4/8/12) are first guesses, tunable in `tuning.json` after on-device play.
The one with real feel impact is AI reaction: it makes the cheap stop a race
against the smart teams, which will make SC/VSC gambles harder than in 1.4.
