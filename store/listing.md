## Full description (max 4000 chars)

You're not the driver. You're the strategist.

Pit Wall puts you on the timing stand for a 12-race championship. Every race
unfolds in real time, sector by sector, on a deterministic simulation where
undercut and overcut, tyre degradation, and track position all emerge from
the model — nothing is scripted. Your job is to read it faster than the
rival pit walls and make the calls that decide the race:

WHEN TO PIT
Track the tyre cliff as it approaches. Pit too early and you lose track
position; too late and the cliff costs you more than the stop ever would.
A live projection shows you exactly where you'd rejoin before you commit.

WHICH TYRE
Soft, medium, or hard — each compound trades pace for durability differently
in every condition. Get caught out by a drying track on the wrong rubber and
you'll be defending, not attacking.

HOW HARD TO PUSH
Push, hold, or conserve — reshape your tyre life and lap time live, lap by
lap, and watch the consequence play out on track immediately.

BUILD YOUR TEAM ACROSS A SEASON
Choose from ten teams and race as one of two drivers per team, from
front-runners to backmarkers each with a distinct car character. Spend
development points between races on power, aero, chassis, and reliability —
the gains you bank early are the gap you're defending by the finale.

TWELVE CIRCUITS, EACH WITH ITS OWN CHARACTER
Street circuits reward the driver over the car. High-speed circuits punish a
mistimed stop harder. Weather shifts mid-race and can flip a strategy on its
head. No two rounds play the same.

BUILT LIKE A BROADCAST
A live timing tower reorders in real time as track position changes hands.
Tyre compounds glow and wear. Gaps tick down in mono digits. The race-start
lights sequence, the pit-stop timer, the tyre-cliff gauge pulsing red as it
approaches — this is a strategy game that looks and feels like Sunday
afternoon at the track.

No driving. No twitch reflexes. Just the calls that separate a podium from a
DNF — and the nerve to make them under pressure.
